# Sales and Use Tax Procedure

This procedure covers United States sales and use tax on purchases. It does not cover
VAT or GST, which are handled by the regional tax teams.

## Tax codes on purchase orders

Every PO line carries a tax code that determines how tax is calculated. Buyers select
the tax code proposed by the tax engine; overriding a proposed tax code requires a
comment and is reviewed monthly by Indirect Tax.

## Exemption certificates

Halvorsen Industries claims the manufacturing exemption for machinery and raw materials
used directly in production in states where it applies. Indirect Tax issues exemption
certificates to suppliers on request; buyers must not sign or send exemption
certificates themselves.

Purchases for offices, IT equipment and maintenance supplies are generally taxable and
are not covered by the manufacturing exemption.

## Use tax self-assessment

When a supplier does not charge sales tax on a taxable purchase, the company owes use
tax. The tax engine accrues use tax automatically at invoice posting when the PO line
has a taxable tax code and the invoice shows no tax. Use tax is accrued at the rate of
the ship-to location.

## Supplier charged tax incorrectly

If a supplier charges sales tax on an exempt purchase, AP requests a corrected invoice.
Tax incorrectly paid in a prior period is recovered by Indirect Tax through a refund
claim and must not be offset against future invoices.

## Record retention

Exemption certificates and tax-relevant invoices are retained for 7 years after the end
of the tax year.
