# Month-End Close and Accruals

The monthly close is completed in five business days (BD1 to BD5) following the last
calendar day of the month. The close calendar is published by the Corporate Controller
at the start of each fiscal year.

## Close calendar

| Day | Activities |
| --- | --- |
| BD1 | Subledger cut-off for AP, AR and inventory; payroll accruals posted |
| BD2 | Expense accruals and intercompany charges posted |
| BD3 | Balance sheet reconciliations due; fixed asset depreciation run |
| BD4 | Management review of variances; top-side adjustments |
| BD5 | Consolidation and close sign-off by the Corporate Controller |

## Expense accruals

Cost center owners must report goods or services received but not yet invoiced when the
amount is USD 10,000 or more per supplier. Amounts below USD 10,000 are not accrued
unless they belong to a recurring service that is accrued every month.

Open POs with a goods receipt but no invoice are accrued automatically by the GR/IR
(goods receipt / invoice receipt) clearing process and must not be accrued manually.

## Manual journal entries

Manual journal entries require a preparer and a separate approver. Entries of USD
250,000 or more also require approval from the Corporate Controller. Supporting
documentation must be attached in the ERP system before approval.

## Balance sheet reconciliations

All balance sheet accounts are reconciled monthly. Reconciling items older than 90 days
must be escalated to the Assistant Controller with a resolution plan.

## Post-close adjustments

After BD5 sign-off the period is closed. Errors discovered later are corrected in the
following period unless the Corporate Controller decides the error is material.
