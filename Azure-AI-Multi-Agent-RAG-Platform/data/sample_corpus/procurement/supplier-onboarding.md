# Supplier Onboarding and Master Data

New suppliers must be onboarded before a purchase order can be issued to them. The
Vendor Master Data Team (VMD) is the only team authorized to create or change supplier
records in the ERP system.

## Onboarding request

The requesting employee submits a supplier onboarding request with the supplier's legal
name, registered address, tax identification number, contact details and the business
reason for adding the supplier. Duplicate checks are run against existing records by
tax identification number and bank account.

## Required documents

- United States suppliers: a signed IRS Form W-9.
- Foreign suppliers: the applicable IRS Form W-8 (usually W-8BEN-E for entities or
  W-8BEN for individuals). See the Withholding Tax Procedure for consequences of a
  missing form.
- A bank letter or voided check confirming the bank account holder name.
- A signed acknowledgment of the Supplier Code of Conduct.

## Due diligence

All suppliers are screened against sanctions and denied-party lists before activation
and again every night. Suppliers with expected annual spend above USD 100,000 also
complete a financial risk assessment and an information security questionnaire.

## Bank detail verification

Bank details are verified by a callback to a phone number already on file or obtained
from an independent source, never from the email or letter requesting the change. The
callback is documented in the supplier record. Payments to new or changed bank accounts
are held for 5 business days after verification.

## Timeline

Standard onboarding takes up to 5 business days after all documents are received.
Expedited onboarding (2 business days) requires approval from the Director of
Procurement and is limited to production-critical needs.

## Inactive suppliers

Supplier records without a transaction for 18 months are blocked automatically. A
blocked supplier must be revalidated, including bank detail verification, before use.
