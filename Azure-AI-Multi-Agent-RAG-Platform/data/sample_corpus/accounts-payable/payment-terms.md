# Payment Terms and Payment Runs

## Standard payment terms

The standard payment term for all suppliers is net 60 days from the invoice receipt date.
Shorter terms require approval from the Treasurer and must be recorded in the supplier
master record before the first PO is issued. Small and diverse suppliers registered in
the Supplier Inclusion Program are paid on net 15 terms.

## Early payment discounts

When a supplier offers an early payment discount (for example, 2 percent if paid within
10 days), AP schedules the payment to capture the discount if the annualized return
exceeds the company's short-term borrowing rate published by Treasury each quarter.

## Payment runs

Payment runs are executed twice a week, on Tuesday and Thursday. Invoices must be
approved and free of blocks by 12:00 noon Central Time on the business day before the
run to be included.

### Urgent payments

Off-cycle payments are allowed only for legal, regulatory or production-critical
obligations. They require approval from the AP Manager and the Treasurer and are limited
to USD 500,000 per payment. Off-cycle requests for convenience are rejected.

## Payment methods

ACH is the default payment method for United States suppliers and wire transfer for
foreign suppliers. Checks are issued only when a supplier cannot receive electronic
payments, and require approval from the AP Manager.

## Payment holds

Payments are held automatically when the supplier is blocked, when bank details changed
within the last 5 business days, or when a withholding tax form is missing or expired.
