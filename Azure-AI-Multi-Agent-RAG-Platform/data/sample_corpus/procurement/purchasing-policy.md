# Purchasing Policy

This policy applies to all purchases of goods and services made by Halvorsen Industries
and its subsidiaries, regardless of funding source. Purchases must be made through a
purchase requisition in the ERP system and converted into a purchase order (PO) before
the supplier begins work or ships goods.

## Purchase requisitions

A requisition must include the supplier, the cost center or project, the general ledger
account, the quantity, the unit price and the requested delivery date. Requisitions for
services must attach a statement of work describing deliverables and acceptance criteria.

Requisitions are routed automatically based on the total value of the order, including
freight and taxes, converted to US dollars at the monthly corporate rate.

## Approval thresholds

| Total order value (USD) | Required approvals |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 5,000 | Cost center manager |
| 5,000.01 to 50,000 | Cost center manager and Finance Controller |
| 50,000.01 to 250,000 | Cost center manager, Finance Controller and CFO |
| Above 250,000 | All of the above and the CEO |

Approvers may not approve requisitions they created. Approval authority cannot be
delegated below the level defined in the Delegation of Authority matrix.

### Split purchases

Splitting a purchase into multiple requisitions or POs to stay below an approval
threshold is prohibited. Procurement reviews POs to the same supplier within a rolling
30-day window and escalates suspected splits to Internal Audit.

## Competitive bidding

Orders above USD 25,000 require at least three written quotes from qualified suppliers,
documented in the sourcing event in the ERP system. Orders above USD 250,000 require a
formal request for proposal (RFP) run by Global Procurement.

### Sole-source justification

A single supplier may be used without competitive bidding only when a sole-source
justification form is approved by the Finance Controller. Acceptable reasons are: the
supplier is the only source of a proprietary item, an emergency affecting safety or
production, or compatibility with existing equipment. Cost alone is not a valid reason.

## After-the-fact purchase orders

A PO raised after the invoice date is an after-the-fact PO. It must be approved by the
Finance Controller regardless of value, and repeated occurrences are reported monthly
to the cost center owner's vice president.
