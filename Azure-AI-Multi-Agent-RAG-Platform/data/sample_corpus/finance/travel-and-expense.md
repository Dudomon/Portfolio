# Travel and Expense Policy

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable and necessary expenses incurred on company
business. Expenses are submitted through the expense module of the ERP system.

## Submission deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date and approved by
the employee's manager within 10 days of submission. Expenses submitted more than 90
days after the expense date are not reimbursed unless the Corporate Controller approves
an exception.

## Receipts

Itemized receipts are required for every expense of USD 75 or more and for all lodging
expenses regardless of amount. A missing receipt declaration may be used once per
quarter, for amounts up to USD 200.

## Air travel

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for
flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires approval from a senior vice
president. Flights must be booked through the corporate travel agency at least 14 days
in advance when possible.

## Meals and per diem

Meals while traveling are reimbursed at actual cost up to the daily limit published by
the travel desk for each city. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client
entertainment events.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel more than four times a year must use the corporate card for travel
expenses. Personal expenses on the corporate card are prohibited and must be repaid
within 15 days. Corporate card transactions must be reconciled in an expense report
every month.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Examples include traffic fines, personal entertainment, upgrades not covered by this
policy, and loyalty program fees.
